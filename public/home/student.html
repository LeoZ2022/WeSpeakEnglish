how to start

  • Step 1

    Sign up to set up your account

  • Step 2

    Search your tutor on the list and pay for the class.

  • Step 3

    Download chatting APP here https://www.classin.com/en/download.html and install it on your PC, tablet or smart phone. Sign up with your phone number in case you forget the password.

  • Step 4

    Set Allow notification for this APP in your smart phone (if you want to use your phone as chatting device)

  • Step 5

    You will get a notification from the APP. You can open the APP to view the timetable.

how to chat

Download the Student manual here.
Another notification will arrive 10 mins prior to the appointment starts. Open the APP, check your audio device functionality, then wait for your tutor who will host the chat. The tutor can adjust the video box size, draw picture, type word on the board. You can type message to your tutor. Each class can be allowed to extend 20 mins, after that the class will be forced to close.

end the class

If you don’t like to chat with your tutor you can quit without any loss within class starts 10 mins. Don’t quit the class if the class start over 10 mins, otherwise your partner will report complaint against you. Write review for each class as detailed as possible. The review should contain but not limit these topics: Tutor’s attitude, skills, helpful or not…

Choose a tutor

If you want to choose the tutor who you have chatted, you can find your previous partner in the class list in you account. Click the tutor name then check his/her availability. Search new tutor on the tutor list.

Cautions

To be respectful. Be cautious of fraud. We are not responsible for the chat content which may cause any unexpected result such as offending, fraud… Once you meet any disrespectful partner or feel not comfortable with the chatting, you can directly tell your partner your feeling or quit the conversation and report to us. Your partner never knows your phone number or your email from our website or APP unless you tell them.
