Who will be your partner?

Smart, ambitious young college students from non-English speaking area. They are eager to improve their speaking skills. Most of them are looking forward to study in or immigrant to US, UK, Canada , Australia and New Zealand. They are going to take IELTS or TOEFL test for university enrollment or immigration. They pay for chatting with you to hope you, as a native English speaker, can help them improve English skills during chat. how to start

  • Step 1

    Sign up to set up your account

  • Step 2

    Set your time rate and your availability

  • Step 3

    Download chatting APP here https://www.classin.com/en/download.html and install it on your PC, tablet or smart phone. Sign up with your phone number in case you forget the password.

  • Step 4

    Set Allow notification for this APP in your smart phone (if you want to use your phone as chatting device)

  • Step 5

    Wait for the learner choosing you. Once an appointment is set up, you will get a notification from the APP. You can open the APP to view the timetable.

how to chat

Download the manual here.
watch left the video.Another notification will arrive 20 mins prior to the appointment starts. Open the APP, check your audio device functionality, then you will be the tutor to host the chat. As the host, you can adjust the video box size, draw picture, type word on the board, or type message to your partner. Each class can be allowed to extend 20 mins, after that the class will be forced to close.

end the class

If you don’t like to chat with your partner you can quit without any loss within class starts 10 mins. Don’t quit the class if the class start over 10 mins, otherwise your partner will report complaint against you. Write review for each class as detailed as possible. The review should contain but not limit these topics: Fluency and coherence, Lexical resource, Grammatical range and accuracy, Pronunciation.

How to get more learner’s attention?

Get a good review score. You will be scored for each class. The learner evaluates you in terms of attitude, helpful or not, punctuation etc. They will choose tutor based on the review score. Set your availability as long as possible. Set your rate reasonable. Starting from a low level, adjust it higher if you become popular (high review score, more chat appointment) Write a good review to let your partner know where they should improve. They like specific, explicit feedback.

Cautions

To be respectful. Be cautious of fraud. We are not responsible for the chat content which may cause any unexpected result such as offending, fraud… Once you meet any disrespectful partner or feel not comfortable with the chatting, you can directly tell your partner your feeling or quit the conversation and report to us. Any topic related with money transferring, donation, your private information disclosure must be precautioned. Your partner never knows your phone number or your email from our website or APP unless you tell them.
