Welcome to join WeSpeakEnglish!

You are almost ready to meet your overseas partner online. Please take these steps:
  1. Ensure your time zone match your actual location, otherwise, you might be appointed at an unexpected time because the learners are from worldwide countries in various time zone.
  2. Set your availability in your account. No availability means you are invisible to all learners. Our system only shows available time slots outside of 12 hours to learners, so no worries about rushed sessions. They will be booking sessions based on their local time. We recommend checking your email daily to stay updated on any booking requests.
  3. Download and install the specific video chat app En Chat from your smartphone App store(The app may not be listed on the top, please scroll down the app list until find it). Use your email to log in. The password is the same as your account on our website. All chats will happen on this APP. So far, the app is only available on smartphones and tablets. We will develop a PC version in the future.
  4. Wait for a learner to choose you.
  5. Each chat will last 30 minutes. Recommend you enter the chat room 5 minutes prior to the chat start time and write a review afterward.

Tips-how to get more attention?
  • Set your availability as much as possible. Fixed time on consecutive days is recommended.
  • Build up a good reputation(reviews).

To get your tutoring report, go to the "My Chats" page under your account and click "Generate Report" button.

Any questions please don’t hesitate to contact us. Have fun!
