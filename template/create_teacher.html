Here are some tips:
Encourage natural conversation: Treat the session as a friendly conversation between friends. Engage the learner in topics of mutual interest and encourage them to express their thoughts freely.
Active listening: Pay close attention to what the learner is saying and provide positive feedback. Encourage them to speak at their own pace and offer guidance when needed.
Provide constructive feedback: Offer gentle corrections on coherence, pronunciation, grammar, and vocabulary as needed. Focus on helping the learner improve their overall communication skills rather than pointing out every mistake.
Create a supportive environment: Make the learners feel comfortable and confident in expressing themselves. Offer praise for their efforts and celebrate their progress.
Remember, no specific tutoring skills are required, as the goal is to provide a supportive environment for the learner to practice speaking English.
