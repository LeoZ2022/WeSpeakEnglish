Frequently asked questions you may be concerned about:

Who will be my partner(tutor)?
Native English speakers from the US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and other countries. Nice, patient, warmhearted retirees, energetic high school students, and other volunteers. Most of them are not professional tutors but can speak native English with you and they like to talk with overseas learners.

Is the chat free?
Most tutors volunteer to chat with you. Our platform charges $1.5 for each chat to cover our operating costs. Therefore, we could survive and provide sustainable service to all learners. We are dedicated to providing users with a reliable, transparent, helpful, and affordable platform. Unlike other free apps, no ads in our app, and no personal information are collected except your email. Keep in mind that there is no real free stuff in the world, the price is always behind it.
Practice with a native is very efficient to improve your speaking skills. They can give you accurate feedback that a non-native cannot do. Strongly recommend you at least practice one time before the speaking test.

Can I get free chats?
To get more free chats, send your invitation code to invite your friends who want to improve their speaking skills to sign up. Find the code in your account. On a smartphone or tablet, log in to your account, click the right top button(3-solid-line icon), and the INVITATION CODE option will be seen. On the computer, the code is at the left bottom of the account webpage. Copy and send the code to your friends, and both of you will get a reward When they sign up successfully. Invite more, you will get more rewards for free chats.

Can I use a PC to video chat?
No. So far, the app is only available on smartphones and tablets. We will develop a PC version in the future.

Why video chat instead of voice chat?
Because video chat is almost like a real face-to-face conversation. IELTS speaking test is face-to-face as well. You and your partner can see each other’s facial reactions which would make the conversation more natural.

Can I use voice chat only?
Yes. You can turn off the camera anytime and just use audio to chat with your partner but video chat is recommended. Don’t be shy, try the video chat before meeting your speaking test examiner.

What if I am dissatisfied with the tutor I choose?
If you are dissatisfied with the tutor you choose, you can quit the chat within 10 minutes without any charge.
