  • Who is WeSpeakEnglish team?

    A group of Canadians who love to help Native English speakers find fun and help overseas learners practice speaking English at a very low cost.

  • How can a referrer involve?

    Find as more as possible English learners who are eager to improve speaking skills and encourage them to join WeSpeakEngish as learners to chat with native English speakers.

  • How much commission can I earn?

    Your earning is based on how many chats your referral learners make. You can get $0.1 from each successful chat. To get more commission, keep as many active learners as you can.

  • How to redeem my rewards?

    Click withdraw in your account. The funds will go to the Paypal account that you designated in 1 week if your withdrawal request is approved. You can only redeem with an account balance above $50, that is, each redemption cannot be less than $50; the interval between two redemptions must be greater than 2 weeks.

  • Who are the prospective learners?

    Any English learner who is eager to practise speaking English. In particular, IELTS and TOFEL examinees.

  • What is the benefit for learners?

    Practise speaking English at very low cost with native English speakers. Efficient way to improve speaking skills.

  • What are the requirements for the learners to video chat?

    1、Willing to chat with English speakers

    2、Have a smartphone

    3、Can access the internet and check email on a daily basis

    4、Sign up as a learner. Find a tutor and pay the chats

    5、Download and install a chat APP En Chat on their smartphone

  • How to ensure the learners register under my account number?

    Make sure they input your account number when they sign up as a learner. Sending the registration link that contains your account number is a good option.
