Where you reside.

Match the country
Select the nearest major country/city name from the list as your time zone. Learners come from various time zone which may differ from yours. If you set the wrong time zone, you would be appointed in an unexpected time slot.
Google "my time zone" to check your real time zone.
For example, London is in UTC 0, New York is in UTC -5, Melbourne is in UTC +10.
Check your email address and ensure it is correct. Otherwise, you will miss the verification email and all notifications.

Self-introduction
Introduce yourself either in audio or in text to let your partner know about you. Just choose one method. The audio is recommended.

Audio
No more than 1 minute.

iPhone:
 Open Voice Memos->Click red button to record->Stop->click left bottom 3 blue dots->Share…->Save to Files->On My iPhone->choose a file folder to save->Add->saving record audio file is done.
Back to the sign-up webpage, click Upload my audio->Browse->On My iPhone->choose the folder you saved the audio file->choose the file you want to upload->Done.

Android:
 The method may vary depending on the models.
Open the recorder to record your voice then save it to a folder. Back to the sign-up webpage, click Upload my audio->Choose file->Browse-> choose the folder you saved the audio file->choose the file you want to upload->Done.
